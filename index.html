About

A community-based workplace safety education initiative started after identifying gaps in safety awareness among temporary foreign workers and South Asian tradespeople through firsthand experience.

We developed and translated illustrated injury prevention resources focused on site-specific safety planning and common workplace hazards across multiple trades in British Columbia.

Fall Prevention

  • Inspect ladders, scaffolds, and work platforms before use.
  • Keep walkways clear of tools, debris, and loose materials.
  • Use guardrails or appropriate fall protection where required.

Head Injury

  • Wear approved head protection wherever required.
  • Inspect helmets before each use.
  • Keep clear of suspended loads and moving machinery.

Power Tools

  • Inspect tools, guards, blades, discs, and cords before use.
  • Never remove or bypass safety guards.
  • Secure work materials using appropriate clamps or supports.

Respiratory

  • Identify potential airborne hazards before starting work.
  • Use wet cutting methods or effective dust extraction where appropriate.
  • Never disturb suspected asbestos-containing materials without the required assessment, training, and controls.

Occupational Eye Safety

  • Choose protective eyewear suited to the task and hazard.
  • Inspect eyewear for cracks, scratches, or other damage before use.
  • Never remove eye protection while exposed to a hazard.
